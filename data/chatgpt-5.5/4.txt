# Luganda Conversation Dataset — Batch 4

**Conversations 076–125 &#124; 50 conversations**

Each conversation begins with warm greetings and contains 5–8 exchanges between the User and Helper. Topics include daily life, education, relationships, business, culture, travel, and practical problem-solving.
