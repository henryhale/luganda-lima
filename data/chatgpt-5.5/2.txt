# Luganda Conversation Dataset — Batch 2

**Conversations 006–025 &#124; 20 conversations**

Each conversation contains 5–8 exchanges, begins with warm greetings, and uses natural Luganda between a User and Helper.

## Conversation 006: Ku mirimu (Work)

**User\:** Gyebale ko, mukwano\! Oli otya leero?

**Helper\:** Gyebale ko naawe\! Ndi bulungi nnyo. Ggwe oli otya?

**User\:** Nange ndi bulungi. Olunaku lwo ku mulimu lutambula lutya?

**Helper\:** Lutambula bulungi, naye nnina emirimu mingi egy'okukola.

**User\:** Okozesa ngeri ki okumaliriza emirimu egyo?

**Helper\:** Nteekateeka emirimu okusinziira ku bukulu bwagyo era ngimaliriza gumu ku gumu.

**User\:** Ekyo kirungi. Nange nja kukigezesa.

**Helper\:** Kirungi nnyo\! Okuteekateeka emirimu kiyamba okukendeeza ku buzibu.

## Conversation 007: Obulamu n'okwekuuma (Health)

**User\:** Wasuze otya, ssebo? Nsuubira oli bulungi.

**Helper\:** Wasuze otya naawe\! Ndi bulungi. Ggwe oli otya?

**User\:** Ndi bulungi, naye mbadde mpulira obukoowu.

**Helper\:** Nsonyiwa okuwulira ekyo. Obadde ofuna ekiwummulo ekimala?

**User\:** Nedda, mbadde nsula essaawa ntono nnyo.

**Helper\:** Gezaako okufuna essaawa ez'okwebaka ezimala, onywe amazzi, era ofune ekiwummulo.

**User\:** Webale nnyo. Nja kugoberera amagezi go.

**Helper\:** Kale. Bw'oba obukoowu bweyongera, weetegereze era weebuuze ku musawo.
