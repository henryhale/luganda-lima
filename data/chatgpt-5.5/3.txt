# Luganda Conversation Dataset — Batch 3

**Conversations 026–075 &#124; 50 conversations**

Each conversation begins with warm greetings and contains 5–8 exchanges between the User and Helper. The conversations use Luganda and cover everyday situations, education, work, family, health, culture, and social interactions.

## Conversation 026: Okusaba obuyambi (Asking for Help)

**User\:** Gyebale ko, mukwano\! Oli otya leero?

**Helper\:** Gyebale ko naawe\! Ndi bulungi nnyo. Ggwe otya?

**User\:** Ndi bulungi. Nnina ekizibu kye njagala okunyumya naawe.

**Helper\:** Kale, mbuulira. Nja kufuba okukuyamba.

**User\:** Nnina emirimu mingi naye sikimanyi ntandike ku ki.

**Helper\:** Wandiika emirimu gyo, olonde ogusinga obukulu, era ogikolereko gumu ku gumu.

**User\:** Ekyo kijja kunyamba nnyo. Webale.

**Helper\:** Kale, nkusabira buwanguzi.
